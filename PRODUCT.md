# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Delegated, deploying to Netlify: plain static HTML/CSS (with minimal JS where needed). It needs no build step and Netlify serves it as is. Revisit if the site outgrows it, for example many work entries needing templating.

## Users
Industry peers and collaborators (producers, agents, other creatives) who review the owner's work and decide whether to get in touch or work together. Visitors are not employers, clients or the general public.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio showing the owner's work in writing/screenwriting, music/audio and video/film. Success: a visitor sees the range and quality of the work and reaches out to collaborate.

## Positioning
Open decision: one person working across screenwriting, music and film. Confirm what ties these together before writing positioning copy.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse the work and then make contact. Likely formats include scripts, tracks or mixes, and video, but the actual pieces have not been supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site hosted on Netlify.
- Three disciplines to present: writing/screenwriting, music/audio, video/film.
- Open decisions: contact method, which pieces to feature, whether media is hosted on the site or embedded from elsewhere, name/branding to use.

## Brand Commitments
None stated yet. Name, voice and identity assets are open decisions.

## Evidence on Hand
None supplied yet. Do not fabricate credits, projects, press or testimonials. The owner's local folders suggest source material (Final Draft and Scenarist scripts, Image-Line and Zenbeats music projects, Blackmagic video projects), but nothing has been selected for publication.

## Product Principles
- Let the work lead: the pieces matter more than the interface around them.
- Show range across disciplines without making the site feel like three separate sites.
- Make getting in touch effortless for a peer who has just seen something they like.
- Use only real, owner-supplied work and credits.
